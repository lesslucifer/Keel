The problem

The router and the watchdog run outside any sandbox, with your rights. Their only limit is one guard script. Nowhere says, in one place, what each may do or what a widening would expose. Three texts list its commands by hand, and they drifted.

What you get

  • One table per session: each limit, and what widening it exposes.
  • A test fails when the tables, the guard, the settings or the launcher disagree.
  • No new power for either.

Beside W5

W5 (resume and reuse) edits the same guard, settings, launcher, router skills, tests and decisions. W5 merges first. W7 builds its tests and decision now. Then it merges the epic in, adds W5's new commands to the tables, and only then stages its owner files. Cost: your W7 commit waits for W5's merge. No second commit, no second review.

How you approve

  1. Answer or accept the questions below; confirm the plan.
  2. Sign off and merge W5 as its plan says.
  3. One commit of the staged guard, settings, agent file and router skills.
  4. After a fresh review: sign off and merge W7.

How you'll know it worked

  • Each table row matches a guard rule, and each guard rule a row.
