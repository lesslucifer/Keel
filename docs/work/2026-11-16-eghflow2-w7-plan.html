E-GHFLOW2.W7 · Your approval accepts all

Today your approval on GitHub cannot sign a pull request whose review did not pass, or one that changes who may sign. After W7 it can. Approving is the whole decision, and the sign-off records what you approved over.

The problem

Your approval signs most pull requests now. It refuses three kinds: a review that failed or never ran, a review its round limit gave up on, and a change to who may sign. For each, you must go to your Mac and type the sign-off. Plan v1 moved those decisions into extra lines and codes in your approval. You ruled that too complicated: the approve button should accept all.

What changes for you

The pull request hasTodayAfter W7 is on main

The sign-off note says what your approval covered: the review's state, and any change to who may sign. The status on the pull request says it too. The typed commands at your Mac stay as they are.

You decide: say go, or overrule one of the seven open points below.

Example: a WU whose review failed

  1. A WU's code review fails on one finding you think is wrong.
  2. You open its pull request on your phone. The build, tests and docs are green. The gate is green too, and its page says "the review did not pass: your approval decides", with the finding.
  3. You approve.
  4. The sign-off is written. Its note records "approved over a failed review". The sign-off status turns green: "Signed by approval, over a review that did not pass".
  5. GitHub merges.

The work

On a Keel pull request on GitHub, an approval of the head commit by a github-tier signer that the base's committed roster lists signs the tip even when its review failed, is missing, reviewed other code or was waived by the round limit, and even when the range changes the roster; the sign-off note records the review state and the roster lines it covered; every other check still blocks the merge.

A · The writer signs over the review and the roster

In short: keel ci sign-off stops refusing for the review and for a roster change. It still requires everything else green. It records what it covered in the note.

B · The gate counts the review as covered

In short: on GitHub the gate runs before you approve, and does not run again when a note arrives. So it treats the review the way it treats the sign-off today: judged by keel/sign-off. On your Mac, and in the epic's walk, a review is covered when an approval signed that tip.

#ItemHow
B1Mark the review's failuresEach failure from the review checks is marked as the review's: the missing or unreadable cr note (still printed under 1·notes), 3·review, 4·review-base, 5·diff-binding. The ids and texts do not change.
B2On GitHubci::red_before_sign_off ignores the review's failures, as it ignores 8·manual-review. keel/gate is green when only those fail. Its page says "the review did not pass: the owner's approval decides", and lists the failures.
B3On your MacIn gated(): when check 8 passes on a note by approval (github tier, carrying a review), the review's failures become WAIVED lines: "review covered by the approval of <signer> (review <id>)". A typed note never covers them.
B4The walk (check 7)A WU seam red only on its review is covered when check 8 accepts an approval note on that WU's tip, judged against the seam's first parent, as the pre-push walk judges a seam. Otherwise the epic stays red, as today.
B5Check 8For a note by approval only, the waiver rule (waiver_drift) is not applied: the approval covers the review as a whole, whatever it says later. Typed notes keep the rule unchanged.
B6The receiptdod/v1 records review: "COVERED", and in waiver the approval's signer and review id.

C · The status says what you approved over

In short: scripts/ci-sign-off.sh reads covers from the note it pushed and adds it to the green status, in fixed words. Nothing else in it changes.

  • "Signed by approval (review N)", then ", over a review that did not pass" when covers.review is set, and ", with a roster change" when covers.roster is.
  • The state itself is one of A3's five words, checked against that list before use; any other value becomes "an unknown review state".

D · Skills

In short: the skills learn that an approval accepts all. Both skill trees change the same way; the patch is yours to apply, as in W4.

  • keel-workflow §5 and §6: on GitHub, your approval covers the review and a roster change, and the note records it. The typed waiver flags stay, for your Mac.
  • keel-router: a pull request with a failed review is still put to you, saying so plainly. The router never approves and never asks you to approve something whose review failed without saying it failed.
  • project-router: the GitHub sign-off sequence loses the typed steps for these cases.

Why this is safe

QuestionAnswer
Who can approve?Only a GitHub id that the base branch's committed roster lists under the github tier: today, you (5499546). Never a key holder, who signs by typing. Never by the head's roster, so a branch cannot add its own signer. The approval must be your latest decision and be on the head commit. A change request by any listed id blocks.
Why may a failed review no longer block?Your ruling: the review advises you, and you take responsibility for what you approve. The gate still shows the failure on the pull request before you approve, and the note records it after.
What still blocks a merge on GitHub?Every required check but the review. keel/ci (the build and the tests), keel/docs, and keel/gate on everything else: the test note and its layer, the doc note, the walk, the hooks. keel/sign-off until a listed id approves. A required result report that is missing, or only in a note. A base that already holds the tip. A sign-off already there that check 8 refuses. A base with no roster.
Does a red build still block?Yes. keel/ci, keel/gate and keel/docs stay required, and none of their workflows changes.
Can the gate going green on a failed review be abused?Only keel/sign-off then stands between it and a merge, and it is required. The writer is its only setter. It turns green only for a new approval (A1), an approval GitHub still lists (A5), or a typed sign-off whose review is green (A6).
What new trust does W7 add?None on GitHub: no workflow, permission, token or setting. The writer still runs main's code. It gets tighter there: an approval note is checked against GitHub's own list (A5), closing one item of backlog G7 on GitHub. On your Mac nothing changes: a same-user shell could already write a github-tier note (G7) or a passing cr note, so a cover opens nothing new. DEC-48 records it at the close.
