E-CRLEVEL · A WU review and an epic review each get their own schedule

Your request of 26 November: a WU review gets one round; the epic review keeps today's schedule.

The problem

One review schedule serves every review. A WU review and an epic review get the same rounds. You want a shorter WU review: one round, whose findings, blockers too, are fixed rather than left open. The epic review stays as it is.

Example: two WUs, one round each

  1. WU A's round 1 reads the whole work and finds two medium findings.
  2. The author fixes them and records the fix. No round 2 runs.
  3. WU B's round 1 finds a blocker. The author fixes it and records the fix, the same way.
  4. At sign-off you see B's fix and the files it changed. Nobody reviewed it again: that is the cost you accepted.
  5. The epic review then runs today's schedule.

Terms

LevelWhich review it is. Work that merges into main gets an epic review; work that merges into an epic gets a WU review.
Shared settingsToday's review settings. They apply to both levels unless a level sets its own.
Recorded fixA fix written down against the round it answers, with no new review.
Blocker fix settingNew, off unless set: from the round it names, blockers too get a recorded fix.

How you'll know it worked

  • In Keel, a WU review stops after round 1 and its blockers get a recorded fix; an epic review runs today's schedule.
  • A project that sets nothing new behaves exactly as today.
  • A bad value at any level is refused at its commit and when a review starts.
  • Work reviewed before this epic still merges.
  • The epic test runs several schedules through the real hooks.

Not in this epic

  • New defaults: one set serves both levels.
  • A level for a single commit's review.
  • What reviewers look for, or your own waiver.
  • Re-reviewing anything already merged.
