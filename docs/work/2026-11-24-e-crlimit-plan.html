E-CRLIMIT · A code review stops after 3 rounds

Your ruling of 24 November: round 2 reads only the fix, and only a blocker earns round 3.

The problem

A review may run 5 rounds today. Rounds 1 and 2 both read the whole work, so one failed round costs two full reviews. A medium finding can still earn rounds 3 and 4. You ruled a shorter schedule.

What changes for you

Example: a review that needs all 3 rounds

  1. Round 1 reads the whole work and finds a blocker.
  2. The author fixes it. Round 2 reads only that fix and finds another blocker.
  3. The author fixes it. Round 3 reads only that fix and still finds a blocker.
  4. The review stops. The tool records it as waived, with the open finding.
  5. You read the finding at sign-off and accept the waiver, or not.

Had round 2 found only medium findings, the author would fix them, record the fix, and the review would end there.

Medium findingMust be fixed, but may be fixed without another review.

How you'll know it worked

  • Round 2 reads only the fix of round 1.
  • After a round 2 with no blocker, the tool refuses round 3, and the recorded fix merges.
  • A blocker still open in round 3 stops the review; you decide at sign-off.
  • Work reviewed under the old schedule still merges.
  • The epic test runs this flow through the real hooks.

Not in this epic

  • What reviewers look for, or how severe a finding is.
  • The waiver and sign-off rules, beyond the round numbers they name.
  • Re-reviewing anything already merged.
