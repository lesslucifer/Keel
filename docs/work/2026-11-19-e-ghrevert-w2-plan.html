The problem

W1 takes GitHub's flow out of the code. The docs still describe it as the way you work: install tells you to set up the bot, architecture explains the checks, eight decisions and four use cases say the flow is current, and the backlog still owes it live checks. Once W1 lands, a reader who trusts them is told to approve on GitHub, which no longer signs anything.

After W2, every record of GitHub's flow is marked replaced by one new decision, that decision says what replaced the flow and what was kept, and install and architecture describe only the typed sign-off, the merge on your Mac and your push.
