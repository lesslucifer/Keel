E-WSANDBOX · What a worker may do, and when it is reused

A review of the limits on background workers (local ports, app packages, websites, the automatic reviewer), and bringing a stopped worker back.

T3 · epic plan · 2026-11-24, amended 2026-11-26 · Keel · its data file, 2026-11-24-e-wsandbox-plan.yaml, sits beside this page

The problem

Workers keep stopping on their own limits, and you do the step by hand. They cannot open a port, so you ran the app's end-to-end test and 14 tests are off. They cannot finish installing packages, so you installed them. The automatic reviewer refused 36 worker commands, many harmless. A stopped worker never comes back: a fresh one starts at about 37k tokens and re-reads its files.

Terms

WorkerA background session doing one part in its own folder.
SandboxThe fence around a worker's shell: what it may write, which websites it reaches.
ReviewerThe model that approves or refuses each worker command outside its fixed list.
LaneA step you run by hand: one command, one log.
ContextWhat a worker has read and said so far; cheapest to reuse while cached ("warm").

What you decide

#QuestionAnswer, and why
1A local port for a worker?Not by default; a part only if W1 shows it stays on this machine.
2Who runs the 14 switched-off tests?You, in a lane, when an epic touches the alert sender.
3Should a worker install app packages?Locked versions only, if W1 gets past a write block. Adding one stays yours.
4May the reviewer allow a website for one command?No, if the tool can switch it off: what a worker reads it could send.
5How do we stop harmless refusals?One fixed scratch helper the reviewer no longer judges.
6How does a stopped worker come back?One launcher command, every limit rebuilt. Stop stays if your probe shows history survives; else a new retire command.
7Are the reuse ceilings settings?Yes: two config keys, 200k and 400k.
8When is a cache warm?Under an hour since the last turn; W6 confirms.
9Who sends a fix to a fresh worker?The router, by your rule; the author may ask. Yours: may the author keep a fix past a ceiling?

How you'll know it worked

  • W1 settles every unchecked fact, with raw output.
  • Every ruling is recorded with its exposure; none gives a worker your keys.
  • A fresh worker's probes match your rulings.
  • A resumed worker remembers its work and still cannot write outside its folder.
  • The router shows each worker's context and cache; no skill contradicts your rules.

Not in this epic

  • Tightening the shared git folder.
  • GitHub's tool in a worker.
  • The router's own limits; the other host program.
  • Packaging the app.
  • Reviews: a reviewer stays fresh every round.
