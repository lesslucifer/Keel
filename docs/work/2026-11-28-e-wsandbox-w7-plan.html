The problem

The router and the watchdog run with your rights, outside any sandbox. One guard script limits them. The router hit that guard 83 times this month, and some of that work went to workers. You cannot add abilities without first seeing today's limits.

Three small fixes: one settings line is wider than the guard; the agent file's command list is out of date; the watchdog reads your local settings file, which does not exist today. My advice: fix the first two, leave the third. Both sessions stay outside a sandbox: it would limit only the shell, which the watchdog lacks.

What W7 builds

Only what you pick. Each pick gets a test that fails first, then the change, staged for your one commit. W5 edits the same files, so W5 merges first. W7 then merges the epic in and stages on top of it. Cost: your W7 commit waits for W5. No second commit or review. If you pick nothing, W7 ends here.

How you approve

  1. Comment your picks (or none); confirm the plan.
  2. Sign off and merge W5.
  3. One commit of W7's staged files.
  4. After a fresh review: sign off and merge W7.

How you'll know it worked

  • Each pick works, and its near misses are still refused.
  • Nothing you did not pick changed.
