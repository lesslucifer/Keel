E-CRCAP · A code review stops after 3 failed rounds

An example epic plan, written by the writing rules.

T3 · epic plan

The problem

A code review can loop without end. Each round (one review run) finds something new. The author fixes it. The next round finds more. One piece of work ran 7 rounds until you stopped it. And when you waive a review (let it merge without a pass), the record still says PASS. This epic stops a review after 3 failed rounds and records a waiver as a waiver.

What changes for you

Example: a review that will not pass

  1. Round 3 fails.
  2. The tool refuses round 4 and lists your three choices.
  3. You say "waive it: the finding is cosmetic".
  4. The review is recorded as WAIVED, with your reason.
  5. You accept the waiver and sign off.

The work

W2 needs W1: its choices include a waiver. W3 can run any time.

W1 · A waived review says WAIVED

In short: the agent records your waiver with your reason. The merge check accepts it and prints it. Sign-off needs your explicit yes.

  • keel notes write cr --verdict WAIVED --reason "…". An empty reason is refused.
  • Sign-off refuses a waived commit without --accept-waiver.
  • A code change after the waiver undoes it.

Your ruling on waivers · UC-REV-2 (new).

Integration: a waiver without a reason is refused; with one, the merge check passes and prints it; sign-off without the flag is refused. Unit: the flag parsing.

W2 · The 3-round limit

In short: preflight counts the failed reviews on the branch. At 3 it refuses a 4th and lists your choices.

  • The count comes from the review notes git already holds. No new state.
  • [review] max_rounds in the config, default 3.

Your ruling: 3 rounds, then you decide · UC-REV-3 (new).

Integration: after 3 failed rounds, preflight refuses and lists the choices; a passing round is not counted. Unit: the count.

W3 · What counts as a nit

In short: two cases never fail a review: a missing test for code that already works, and a tidy-up of test helpers. Text only.

  • The review rubric names both cases.

Details

Acceptance

  • A 4th review round is refused, and the message lists the three choices.
  • A waived review reads WAIVED with its reason, in the note and in the merge check's output.
  • Sign-off on waived work needs an explicit acceptance.
  • A code change after a waiver undoes it.
  • The epic test passes.

A scratch repo, no network:

  1. Three review rounds fail. The 4th is refused.
  2. Your waiver is recorded. The merge check passes and prints it.
