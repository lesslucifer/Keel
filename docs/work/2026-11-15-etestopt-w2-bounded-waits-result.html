Deviations

  • The bound helper has no separate unit test, as the plan said: the watcher test asserts each "never" wait gives up no sooner than the bound it was given and well before 10 s.
  • Not in the plan: before asking for poll 2 or the output, the test waits for poll 1 under the full bound. That way the shims whose exit it checks are known to have started, and the pid check cannot pass because nothing ran.
