Differences from the plan

  • A stuck program is stopped with all its children. The planned way stopped only the program itself.
  • The page has more buttons, to pick a tool or a project. Without them, nobody could check the two pickers live.
  • More guard tests. Only the app's two entry files may load Electron. Every package has one exact version.
  • Your rules change took two commits; the second fails the check on empty test folders.
  • Small build fixes, and six type errors in early tests fixed; no test changed what it checks.
  • The worker could not rebuild the app: the sandbox refused the Electron download.
  • Reviews 1 to 3 failed; all their findings are fixed, each with a test that failed first. Among them: a startup job in your shell could stall the app, and an older or non-English git could add no project.
