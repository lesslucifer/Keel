E-APPLIST · Alerts and your list

The first part of the local app: an alert at your desk and one list of what waits on you. It only reads.

T3 · epic plan · 2026-11-20 · Keel · its data file, 2026-11-20-e-applist-plan.yaml, sits beside this page · the design: local-app.html

The problem

What waits on you reaches you by Telegram or the router's terminal, or you ask the router. Nothing alerts you at your desk. Nothing lists what waits on you in one place.

Replanned on your rulings of 20 November: the app's build decisions.

Example: a plan to read

  1. A worker finishes a plan.
  2. The router notes "plan to read" and messages you.
  3. Your Mac shows an alert. You click it.
  4. The list shows the plan. You click Open and read it.
  5. You approve it to the router. The item leaves the list.

Terms

Update logA file where the router writes one line per change the app needs. Only the router and its watchdog write it.
WatchdogThe session that checks the workers every 5 minutes.
Router not heardThree watchdog checks passed with no line.
Browser modeThe window drawn in a browser on made-up data.

How you'll know it worked

  • A router alert shows once, and its click opens the item, even after a restart.
  • Each item leaves the list when its sign-off, merge or router line appears.
  • Open shows the committed page, not a newer working file.
  • After 15 silent minutes, the app says the router is not heard.
  • With the app closed, everything works as today.
  • The epic test drives the real window, from a router line to the item and out.

Not in this epic

  • Any button that signs, merges, commits or pushes (the next app epic).
  • The fleet, epic and worker screens; many projects; alerts when away.
  • Any change to keel itself.
  • A menu-bar icon, a menu, a start at login.
