How to read it

  • The root is your needs. Each branch is one need, grouped by rank: must, should, later.
  • Under each need sit the jobs it rests on, as the result's needs table lists them. Each job shows its number and what the app does with it (the legend is below the maps).
  • A job can sit under two needs. Jobs no need rests on get a branch of their own, so none is lost.
  • The first map shows only the needs. The next four open each rank.

Legend: what the app does with a job

MarkMeaningJobs
taken overThe app does the job itself: only watching, alerting and listing. The old way keeps working without the app.4
a buttonThe job stays where it is. The app opens its doc or copies its command.4
left aloneThe job stays as it is. The app may show its result.25
droppedA piece of GitHub's flow that goes away. Something on your Mac covers it.15

Numbers: n a need, j a job for you, g a GitHub job, r a job for the router and the workers. Each row, with its file and state, is in the result: your jobs, the router's jobs, the needs.
