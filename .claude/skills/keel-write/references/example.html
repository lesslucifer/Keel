The problem

A code review can loop without end. Each round finds something new, the author fixes it, and the next round finds more. One piece of work ran 7 rounds until you stopped it. And when you waive a review, the record still says PASS.

What changes for you

WhenTodayAfter
A review keeps failingRounds go on until you noticeAfter 3 failed rounds, the tool stops and asks you
You must decideNo defined choicesThree: one more round, waive, or stop
You waive a reviewRecorded as PASSRecorded as WAIVED, with your reason
You sign offNothing shows a waiverYou must accept the waiver

Example: a review that will not pass

  1. Round 3 fails.
  2. The tool refuses round 4 and lists your three choices.
  3. You say "waive it: the finding is cosmetic".
  4. The review is recorded as WAIVED, with your reason.
  5. You accept the waiver and sign off.

What you decide

#QuestionAnswer, and why
1How many failed rounds before you decide?3, changeable in the config. Fewer stops good reviews; more is today's loop.
2Can you waive before any round runs?Yes. Some work needs no review, such as a revert.
3Does a later code change undo a waiver?Yes. A waiver covers only the code it saw.

How you'll know it worked

  • A 4th failed round is refused, and the message lists your three choices.
  • A waived review reads WAIVED, with its reason.
  • Sign-off on waived work asks you to accept the waiver.
  • A code change after a waiver undoes it.
