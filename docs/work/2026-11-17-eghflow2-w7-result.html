What happened

Delivered against the requirement. On a WU's or an epic's pull request, an approval by a signer the base's roster lists signs the tip once every other check is green. It signs whatever the review's state: missing, unreadable, FAIL, stale (a review of other code), or the round limit's WAIVED. It also signs a range that changes the roster. It writes no review note. The sign-off note records the review's state, the review note's hash and the roster lines. The gate on your Mac, the epic's walk and the push check accept the review as covered by that approval. On GitHub the gate leaves the review to the sign-off. A typed sign-off gets no cover. A task's pull request is untouched.
