Every field on the six screens, and its one source: persistent, or the router.

T3 · a page of the W3 result · 2026-11-18 · revision 4, on your comment of round 3 · moved here from the result to keep it under the comment server's 100 KB limit

Two sources

Persistent: read from disk or git: docs, branches, keel's notes and read commands, run records, the inbox's files, the app's own files. It survives any restart. Router: told by the router, line by line. The app keeps it in memory only. It is gone when the router restarts, and the app reads it again when it starts. The design: the result, "Two sources".

A field that needs any router value is a router field. When the router is silent, it shows its persistent part, never a blank.
