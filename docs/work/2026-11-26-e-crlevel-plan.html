E-CRLEVEL · A WU review and an epic review each get their own schedule

Your request of 26 November: a WU review gets one round; the epic review keeps today's schedule.

The problem

One review schedule serves every review. A WU review and an epic review get the same rounds. You want a shorter WU review: one round. The epic review stays as it is.

What changes for you

Example: two WUs, one round each

  1. WU A's round 1 reads the whole work and finds two medium findings.
  2. The author fixes them and records the fix. No round 2 runs.
  3. WU B's round 1 finds a blocker. The review stops, recorded as waived.
  4. At sign-off you read the open blocker and accept the waiver, or not.
  5. The epic review then runs today's schedule.

Terms

LevelWhich review it is. Work that merges into main gets an epic review; work that merges into an epic gets a WU review.
Shared settingsToday's three review settings. They apply to both levels unless a level sets its own.
Blocker, medium findingBoth must be fixed before merge; a medium one may be fixed without a new round.

What you decided

#QuestionYour ruling, 26 November
1How are the levels written?A WU section and an epic section, each taking any of the three settings.
2Which value wins?The level's, then the shared one, then the default, one setting at a time.
3What is one WU round?The WU cap set to 1, alone. Medium findings get a recorded fix. Still open: what a blocker gets. Your comment asks that it be fixed, not waived; that changes the waiver rule, so it waits for your choice.
4How does the tool know the level?From where the work merges.
5Where does your value go?Keel's own rules file, in your last W2 commit, after you reinstall keel.
6Do this epic's own WU reviews get one round?No. The setting is committed last.

How you'll know it worked

  • In Keel, a WU review stops after round 1; an epic review runs today's schedule.
  • A project that sets no level behaves exactly as today.
  • A bad value at either level is refused at its commit and when a review starts.
  • Work reviewed before this epic still merges.
  • The epic test runs each level's schedule through the real hooks.

Not in this epic

  • New defaults: one set serves both levels.
  • A level for a single commit's review.
  • What reviewers look for, or the waiver and sign-off rules.
  • Re-reviewing anything already merged.
