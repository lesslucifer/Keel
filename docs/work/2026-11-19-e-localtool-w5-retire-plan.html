The problem

You dropped GitHub's flow. Its pieces still run: the checks, the sign-off by approval, auto-merge, the bot's pull requests. On 2 December GitHub changes how such checks run, which may break them before anyone removes them. The four earlier designs also left about thirty follow-ups, and none has an epic yet. W5 decides what goes when, and in which order the app is built.
