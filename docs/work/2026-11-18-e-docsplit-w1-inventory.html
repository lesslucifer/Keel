What each slimmed doc drops

  • Decisions: lineage rows go. A replaced record keeps only "DEC-n · title · replaced by DEC-m". A live record keeps its choice, its why and what was rejected, about 250 words at most. Ids are never reused.
  • Use cases: the build history goes ("built by", "amended by"). The contracts stay, as data.
  • Backlog: done and struck rows go as it becomes data. Their ids are never reused.
  • Architecture and install: history goes, and any rule a skill already holds becomes a pointer to that skill.
  • Every slimmed doc: every id cited in code, skills or docs still resolves. Git keeps what went.
