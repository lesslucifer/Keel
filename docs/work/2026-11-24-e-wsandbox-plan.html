W1 settled the facts; on 26 November you ruled every point. This plan records what gets built, in what order, and what stays open.

Terms

ReviewerThe model that approves or refuses commands outside the fixed list.
ContextWhat a worker has read and said; cheapest to reuse while cached ("warm").

What you decide

Ruled 26 November: ports on any address; packages by pnpm or cargo; no site list; scratch through one helper; the 14 tests deleted; parts 2–4 in one commit.

#Open questionAnswer first, and why
1May a worker change another worktree of the same repo?Not yet: it changes the file guard and a decision.
2Do the router and the watchdog stay outside a sandbox?Yes, the guard is the bound: a sandbox would stop it running the launcher.
3How does a stopped worker come back?One launcher command, limits rebuilt: your probe decides what stop keeps.
4Are the reuse ceilings settings?Two config keys: 200k cold, 400k warm.
5When is a cache warm?Within its last cache lifetime: an hour for a 1-hour write; W6 confirms.
6Who sends a fix to a fresh worker?The router, by your rule. Yours: may the author keep a fix past a ceiling?

How you'll know it worked

  • Each ruling is recorded with its cost and bound; W1's live confirmations ran before W2 and W4 built on them.
  • A fresh worker's probes match the rulings: it binds a port, adds a package, and a site is the reviewer's call.
  • A resumed worker remembers its work and still cannot write outside its folder.
  • The router and the watchdog each have one table, and the guard test pins it.
