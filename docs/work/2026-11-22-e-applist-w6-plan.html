The app shows what waits on you, and opens each plan or result as committed.

T3 · WU plan · 2026-11-22 · Keel · its data file, 2026-11-22-e-applist-w6-plan.yaml, sits beside this page · the epic: E-APPLIST plan

The problem

The app alerts you, but its window shows only your settings. You still ask the router what waits on you, and open plans by the path it gives.

What you get

  • Waits on you, the screen the app opens on. Three groups: yours, not yours (a worker acts), and sent.
  • Items arrive and leave by themselves: a result to sign, then to merge; a plan to read; a router question; a worker waiting on you; main not pushed; a task to merge into its work unit.
  • Open: the plan or result exactly as committed, in your browser, even while someone edits the file.
  • Mark sent on a plan or question you answered. It survives a restart.
  • A click on an alert shows that work unit's items. The alert names the project.

How it works

  1. The router sets a work unit's stage to "plan to read".
  2. The plan appears under Yours, with its tip and time.
  3. You click Open. The app copies the committed docs into its own folder and opens the page in your browser.
  4. You tell the router "approved" and click Mark sent. The item moves to Sent.
  5. The router writes "plan approved". The item leaves.

When the router is silent, a bar says so, and its items hide.

What it makes

  • The list and its rules, in the app's main part.
  • Screen 2, a tab bar, and two fields on the Projects screen: the counts and the last change. All in the chosen look.
  • Open and Mark sent. Neither writes into your project.
  • Sample data for each item, to see the screen in a browser first.

Nothing is built or packaged. The epic test and the signed-app checks wait for W9.

How you'll know it worked

  • In browser mode, every item draws in its group, and the buttons answer.
  • Running on your Keel checkout, the list shows what really waits.
  • Open shows the committed text after you edit the file.
  • A sent mark is still there after a quit and restart.
