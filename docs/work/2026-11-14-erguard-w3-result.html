Liveness: nothing replaces it

Decided as the task preferred. A worker that posts nothing is invisible: a wedge and a long turn are the same silence, so a clock adds a model pass, not information. No reminder, schedule, loop or DSH watchdog is armed. The router reads worker_status and the transcript at its next wake; while a watcher is armed its bound is the longest silence (110 minutes at most), and with none armed only the owner's next message. That residual is written into DEC-57 and the skill, not hidden. The armed watcher's own bounded exit is not a liveness check: it is the wake the channel already has.
