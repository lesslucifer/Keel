What happened

Delivered as ruled, with one narrowing. Pushing an epic branch after its merge passes. A push mid-close is judged on the last commit's epic evidence. An archive commit followed by a review fix passes the epic gate, the merge into main and the push, with no evidence rewritten. The narrowing: these close commits are not held to the rule that no working doc is tracked; that rule stays with the epic's last commit.
