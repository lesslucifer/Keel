What happened

How you'll know: met or not

  • Met. Short forms of 7 to 12 characters pass; 6, 13 and a later code change fail.
  • Met. The epic's gate shows its 10 walk steps green, with no waiver.
  • Met. Keel's checks pass. One app test is skipped here because the sandbox blocks file watching. It runs on your Mac.

Differences from the plan
